The learning platform holds your live classes, recorded courses and test series. Access is switched on with an activation code from us.

Log in to the learning platform

First time here

  1. Enrol. Choose a program and complete enrolment with Infiante360 Tech Academy.
  2. Get your activation code. We send the code for your plan or batch to your registered email or WhatsApp.
  3. Log in. Open the learning platform and sign in or create your account.
  4. Activate. Enter the activation code to unlock the courses, recordings and tests in your plan.
  5. Learn and practise. Join live classes, watch recordings, attempt tests and use the simulators.

Need a code or help?

Message us with the email you enrolled with and your program or batch. Never send passwords or payment details.
