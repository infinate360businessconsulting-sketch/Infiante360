# Meta ads compliance and creative checklist (verify against Meta's current Advertising Standards before launch)

Landing page: https://infiante360techacademy.com/lp/enterprise-martech-demo.html

## Policy checks built into the page
- Ad text, creative and page say the same thing: a live demo, registration required, no guaranteed outcome.
- No personal-attribute copy ("Are you struggling with...", age, health, finances, job loss). Copy addresses the business and role, not the person.
- No guaranteed jobs, salaries, revenue or rankings; no before/after claims; no invented clients, endorsements, certifications or partnerships.
- Independence line: "not affiliated with or endorsed by Adobe". Adobe names are used descriptively only; do not use Adobe logos in ads without permission.
- Privacy policy linked beside the form; separate optional consent for email, phone and WhatsApp; opt-out stated; no sensitive data requested; form warns against sending confidential data.
- Page loads fast, has a working form, a visible business name, contact details and a thank-you page.
- Videos: your own session recordings; no client data, no third-party music or footage without a licence; add captions.
- Meta Pixel and Conversions API: add only after a cookie/consent banner is in place for the regions you target (UK, EU, Switzerland, Japan need consent). Pixel ID not yet added.
- Special-category targeting is not used. Lookalikes use consented, qualified registrants only.

## Image placeholders (replace the SVG with your own file of the same name or update the page)
Page images in `assets/placeholders/`: hero 1200x630, presenter 600x600, architecture 1200x675, demo screenshot 1200x675.
Ad images: feed square 1080x1080, Stories/Reels 1080x1920 (keep key text inside the central safe area, under 20% text is no longer enforced but keep text short). Videos: 15s and 30s, 1:1 and 9:16, with captions.
No private or client information in screenshots.

## Form fields (live)
Full name, business email, phone/WhatsApp (optional), job title, company, country, industry, company size, Adobe products used (multi-select), current initiative, preferred demo topic, preferred meeting time, primary business challenge, optional consent for email / phone / WhatsApp, required privacy consent.
