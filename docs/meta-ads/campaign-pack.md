## 3. Geography (separate campaigns, never pooled)
US, UK, Germany, Netherlands, Switzerland, UAE, Singapore, Australia, Japan, India. Localise copy (Germany, Netherlands, Japan: local-language variants reviewed by a native speaker; do not machine-translate unreviewed). Test budgets by CPL and lead quality.
Schedule (local time): India 8-10 AM and 5:30-9 PM IST; UK 8-10 AM and 4-7 PM; US 7-9 AM and 5-8 PM; DE/NL 8-10 AM and 4-6:30 PM; UAE 9-11 AM and 5-8 PM. Run 14 days, then optimise on lead quality and attendance. Note: the live session is 10 PM IST, which suits India best; for other regions offer a recorded or repeat session before spending, or the CTA becomes the architecture discussion.

## 4. Audiences (what Meta supports vs what the form qualifies)
Supported: location, age 28+, interests (Adobe Experience Cloud, Adobe Analytics, customer data platform, marketing automation, digital marketing, data engineering), Advantage+ audience with suggestions, custom audiences (website, video 50%+, FB/IG engagement, lead form, consented customer lists), lookalikes from qualified registrants (seed 100+), exclusions (existing registrants, students, employees, current clients).
Meta job-title and employer targeting is limited and not reliable in all regions. Not supported: filtering by Adobe product use or company revenue. Qualify on the form and in CRM; send only qualified registrants back as the lookalike seed.

## 6. Lead form (landing page + Google Form)
Full name, business email, company, job title, country, industry, company size, Adobe products used, current initiative, main challenge, preferred demo topic, preferred meeting time, separate consent checkboxes for email, phone and WhatsApp, privacy acknowledgement. Reject free-mail domains for the qualified-lead flag (do not block registration).

## 7. Follow-up sequence
See docs/meta-ads/follow-up-emails.md. Triggers: immediate confirmation; T-24h reminder; T-1h joining reminder; post-demo resources plus discovery offer; enterprise: architecture assessment; SI: partnership. Send only to people who consented; include opt-out and postal address.

## 8. Retargeting
Video 50%+ viewers; FB/IG engagers (365 days); site visitors (180 days); landing visitors not registered (30 days); registered not attended (14 days); attended not booked (30 days). Minimum audience size applies; combine small audiences. Use consented lists only.

## 9. Budget
India test: INR 1,500 to 3,000/day (suggest INR 2,000: A 40%, B/C/D 30%, E 15%, retarget 15%). International: start at USD 20 to 40/day per country for 7 days, scale only where cost per qualified lead and attendance justify it. Do not optimise on cheap leads alone.

## 10. 14-day launch
D1-2 pixel/CAPI, UTMs, form, creative; D3 launch A and retarget in India; D4-7 add B to E; D8 first review (CPM, CTR, CPC, CPL, form completion); D9-10 pause weakest 30% of ads, add 2 new; D11 launch one international market; D12-14 review qualified-lead rate and attendance.
## 11. 30-day optimisation
Week 3 shift budget to best persona/angle by qualified lead cost; test hook, format, LP headline; build lookalike from qualified registrants; week 4 add markets, refresh creative, review pipeline and discovery calls, decide scale/stop.

## 12. KPI dashboard
Spend, CPM, CTR, CPC, cost per registration, cost per qualified enterprise lead, demo attendance rate, cost per attended demo, discovery meetings, assessments requested, opportunities, proposals, pipeline value, closed revenue. Review weekly; qualified = Tier 1/2 fit by form.
