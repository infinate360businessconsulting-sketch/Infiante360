# Consent-based review request kit
Rules: ask only people who really attended, learned or worked with you. Never script or edit their wording, never offer money, gifts or discounts for a review (platforms ban incentivised reviews), and accept negative feedback. Never name a client without written permission.

## Email (learners)
Subject: Would you share honest feedback on your Infinite360 session?
Hi [Name],
Thanks for joining [programme / demo session]. If you have two minutes, I'd value your honest feedback, positive or critical, on [LinkedIn recommendation / Google form]: [link]. It's optional, and you're welcome to say no. If you would like your feedback shown on our site, tell us and we will ask for your written permission first.
Thanks, Pavan Babu Gandla

## WhatsApp
Hi [Name], thanks for attending [session]. If you're happy to, could you leave honest feedback here: [link]? Totally optional. +91 82968 93895

## Email (corporate clients)
Subject: Feedback on our work together
Hi [Name], we'd welcome your candid feedback on [engagement], and a short public review on Clutch or LinkedIn only if your company's policy allows it. Please don't share confidential details. We will not publish your name or company without your written approval.

## Where reviews go
LinkedIn recommendations (Pavan, company page), Clutch/GoodFirms (clients only), Academy feedback form. Publish on the site only with written consent, recorded in `content/testimonials.json` with permission date.
