# AI citation benchmark: manual workflow
Questions: `data/seo/ai_citation_benchmark.csv` (100, generated by `scripts/make_benchmark.py`). Result columns are **empty**. No result exists until someone observes one.

## Rules
- Use official APIs only where they offer search plus citations, or test by hand in the product's own interface. Do not automate consumer interfaces or work around limits.
- One row per question per platform per date. Copy the row, fill `platform`, `test_date`, `method` (`api` or `manual`).
- `observed_answer`: short paraphrase, not a long quote. `cited_domains` and `cited_page_url`: exactly as shown. `our_domain_cited`: yes or no.
- `competitor_visibility`: domains cited instead, as observed. `recommended_improvement`: a specific page or fix, or blank.
- Never fill a cell from memory or expectation. Blank means not tested.

## Cadence
Test a sample of 10 questions per platform each month, rotating topics. Platforms: ChatGPT Search, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode.
Citations vary between runs, so treat one observation as a sample, never as a ranking.

## Using results
Group by topic; where competitors are cited and we are not, check whether we have a page for that intent (see `data/seo/cluster_url_map.json`) before writing a new one. Improve an existing page before adding another.
