## Startup Name
Infiante360 Tech Academy (operated by Infiante360 Business Consulting & Tech Academy). Website: https://infiante360techacademy.com

## 1. Who is your customer?
Three groups, served in this order:

1. **Individual professionals.** Software engineers, data and analytics people, QA and support staff, and career switchers in India who want to move into Adobe Experience Cloud and marketing technology roles (Adobe Experience Platform, Real-Time CDP, Journey Optimizer, Customer Journey Analytics, Web SDK) and, increasingly, Agentic AI for MarTech. Most are already employed and learn on evenings and weekends, which is why our live batches run on Sunday evenings and weekends.
2. **Companies and delivery teams.** Learning and development leads, practice heads and system integrators who need their consultants and engineers productive on Adobe tools. They buy corporate training, tailored batches and capability building.
3. **Senior leaders.** Heads of data, marketing technology and digital, who want a short, executive-level programme on customer data architecture and AI (our CXO Hive format).

## 2. What is their problem?
Adobe Experience Platform and its connected products are powerful but hard to learn. In our experience as practitioners (the founder has 15+ years in enterprise data and MarTech delivery):

- Official documentation is wide and deep, but it does not tell a learner what to do first or how the pieces fit in a real project.
- Most courses explain screens and buttons. They do not teach how to design schemas, identities, audiences and journeys the way a delivery team does.
- Learners rarely get a safe place to practise, because the software sits behind enterprise licences.
- Employers want people who have worked on a realistic project and can explain their decisions in an interview.
- Companies struggle to upskill whole teams quickly without pulling senior people off client work.

## 6. How is your solution better than present ones?
We compare by category, without naming or criticising anyone:

| Option today | Gap | What we do differently |
|---|---|---|
| Free videos and documentation | No structure, no feedback, no project | A guided path, mentor feedback and a capstone project |
| Generic online course sites | Broad content, little Adobe depth | Focused on the Adobe stack and Agentic AI for MarTech only |
