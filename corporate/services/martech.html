Marketing Technology

MarTech architecture, Adobe Experience Cloud and personalisation across data, analytics and journeys.

Overview

Marketing technology only pays off when the stack is connected: data, analytics, journeys and content. We help marketing and technology leaders design and implement that stack, with strength across Adobe Experience Cloud.

How we help

  • MarTech stack assessment and rationalisation
  • Adobe Experience Platform, Journey Optimizer and Customer Journey Analytics
  • Web SDK and tagging for reliable data collection
  • Personalisation and journey orchestration design
  • Measurement frameworks and testing plans

Our approach

  1. Review: current stack, data collection and reporting.
  2. Plan: priority journeys and measurement.
  3. Build: data collection, audiences and journeys.
  4. Improve: test, measure and iterate.

Typical deliverables

  • Stack assessment
  • Measurement plan
  • Journey and audience designs
  • Implementation and QA notes

Questions
